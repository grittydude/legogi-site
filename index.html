Features

Everything you need. Nothing you don't.

No bank logins, no wallets, no linked accounts. Just your income and expenses, kept honest.

Add by voice

Say "spent four thousand on lunch" and L'egogi drafts the entry for you to check and save.

Share a receipt

Share a bank transfer receipt from your banking app. The amount, payee and date are read on your phone.

Budgets

Set a monthly limit overall or for each category, and see how much is left at a glance.

Clear insights

See where your money went this month, which categories lead, and who you pay most.

Debts and loans

Track money you lent or borrowed, record repayments, and see what's still owed.

Totals you can trust

Transfer fees are logged for you, money moved to savings doesn't count as spending, and you can export a CSV any time.

How it works

From bank receipt to logged, in seconds.

No typing amounts off a screenshot.

Share

After a transfer, tap Share on the receipt in your banking app and pick L'egogi.

Read on your phone

L'egogi reads the amount, the person you paid and the date. The image never leaves your phone.

Saved

Check the details, pick a category, and save. The transfer fee can be added for you.

Privacy

Your money is your business.

L'egogi keeps your financial records in the app's private storage on your phone. We don't run servers that receive them, and we don't sell or share them.

Read the privacy policy
  • No account or sign-up needed.
  • No bank logins. L'egogi never connects to your bank.
  • Receipts are read on your phone, not uploaded.
  • No ads and no analytics.
